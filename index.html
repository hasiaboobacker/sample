Popular Destinations

Key stops across northern, central, and southern Kerala.

Munnar

Rolling tea plantations, cool mountain air, and misty viewpoints in the Idukki hills.

Alappuzha

Calm backwater canals, traditional kettuvallams (houseboats), and lush paddy fields.

Kochi

Historic Fort Kochi streets, Chinese fishing nets, art galleries, and vibrant cafes.

Wayanad

Forest trails, waterfalls, wildlife sanctuaries, and spice-scented highland estates.

Cultural & Nature Experiences

What makes travelling through Kerala memorable.

  • Ayurveda & Wellness: Traditional herbal therapies and calm retreat stays.
  • Performing Arts: Kathakali, Mohiniyattam, and Kalaripayattu showcases.
  • Malabar & Coastal Cuisine: Appam, puttu, Malabar biriyani, seafood, and filter coffee.
  • Monsoon & Hill Trails: Lush greenery, waterfalls, and scenic drive routes.
